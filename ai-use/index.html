AI Use Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 • Applies to: Zera Studio for Android

1. In Short

Zera Studio uses AI to edit videos that you record or import yourself. It is made for adult creators: you must be 18 or older to use it. This page explains what the AI does, which AI we use, what you may not do with it, and how we keep it safe. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

2. What the AI Does

  • Captions. It listens to the speech in your video and writes timed captions in Hindi, English or Hinglish.
  • AI Edit (B-roll). It watches your talking video and adds B-roll, motion graphics, zooms and captions timed to what you say.
  • Editing assistant. The assistant in the app is an AI. It makes edits to your own project when you ask it to, using the app's editing tools.
  • Pictures and backgrounds. Where available, it can make a picture from your description or a new background behind you in your own footage.

Every AI result opens in the editor, where you can review, change or delete it before you export anything.

3. Which AI We Use

  • The AI features are normally powered by Google's Gemini models, used through Google Cloud (Vertex AI).
  • While Google's AI service is unavailable to us, AI Edit and auto captions run on Anthropic's Claude models (through Anthropic's API) and Amazon Transcribe (speech-to-text, in Amazon Web Services' Mumbai region). AI pictures, AI backgrounds and the editing assistant are paused during this time.
  • The app never talks to the AI directly, and no key inside the app can reach it. Your request goes to our own servers, which call the AI provider on your behalf.
  • Only the media needed for the edit you asked for is sent, and our servers delete it automatically on the schedule in our Privacy Policy (uploads within 14 days).
  • The audio copy sent to Amazon Transcribe is deleted as soon as your words come back, and within one day at the latest. Anthropic does not train its models on what we send and normally deletes it within 30 days.
  • We do not sell your media, and we do not use it to train AI models.

4. Who Can Use It

Zera Studio and its AI features are only for people aged 18 or older, and are not directed to anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it and delete its data.

5. What You May Not Do With the AI

When you use the AI features you must follow Google's Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy, Anthropic's Usage Policy and our Terms of Service. In particular, you may not use the AI to:

  • Create, edit or share any sexual content involving minors. We report it to the authorities.
  • Create sexually explicit content, or intimate, "undress", face-swap or deepfake content of any real person.
  • Impersonate a real person, or make a video that falsely shows someone saying or doing something, to deceive, harass or defraud.
  • Create content that promotes hate, harassment, violence, self-harm, or illegal activity such as scams, fraud or spam.
  • Create misinformation meant to mislead people about facts, health, elections or public safety.
  • Identify, track or profile people from their face or voice.
  • Get around the AI's safety filters, overload the service, collect its outputs in bulk, or use its outputs to build or train other AI models.
  • Use footage, music or images you do not have the rights to.

6. How We Keep It Safe

  • Every AI request belongs to an account. AI features only work when you are signed in, so each request is tied to one user.
  • Limits on use. AI features use credits, and we limit how often each account, and the service as a whole, can run them.
  • Built for your own talking videos. AI Edit works on short videos you upload yourself (up to 3 minutes), not on arbitrary content.
  • The AI provider's own safety filters apply to every AI request.
  • Enforcement. If an account breaks this policy, we stop its AI features or close it, and we report illegal content to the authorities.

7. About AI Results

AI can make mistakes, for example a misheard word in a caption or a B-roll clip that does not fit. Please check your video before you publish it. You are responsible for what you publish.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the product develops. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated Effective Date.

About Zera Studio

Zera Studio is an AI video editing app for Android by ZERA AI. You record or import your own talking video; Zera adds captions, B-roll, music, transitions and a clean voice, and you export the finished reel to your phone. The app uses Google Sign-In only to create your account (your name and email address), and uses AI services (Google Cloud, and Anthropic and Amazon Web Services while Google's AI is unavailable to us) only to understand the speech and footage in the videos you upload so it can edit them.

Zera Studio edits videos; it does not generate people. It has no feature for making sexual, nude, "undress", face-swap or deepfake content, and using it for non-consensual intimate imagery of anyone is banned under our Terms of Service. Zera Studio is for adults aged 18 and over. See our AI Use Policy for how the AI is used and our Privacy Policy for how your data is handled.
