User Rights & Data Controls

Account Deletion Policy & Instructions

Effective Date: October 14, 2026 • Applies to: Zera Studio for Android

1. Overview

Users of Zera Studio for Android have the right to request the deletion of their account and associated personal information.

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, Zera Studio provides both an in-app path to initiate account deletion and an external web/email request mechanism for users who cannot access the mobile application.

2. How to Request Account Deletion

Option 1: In-App Account Deletion (Recommended)

Initiate deletion within the Android app

  1. Open the Zera Studio app on your Android device.
  2. Sign in to the account you wish to delete.
  3. Go to Settings or your Account Profile page.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the account deletion request when prompted.

Upon confirmation, your session will end and account data will be scheduled for permanent removal.

Option 2: Web & Email Support Request

Request deletion if you cannot access the app

If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your Android device, you may submit a manual account deletion request via email:

  1. Send an email from the address registered with your Zera Studio account.
  2. Recipient: support@zera-ai.com
  3. Subject line: Account Deletion Request - Zera Studio
  4. Provide your registered email address and display name or User ID in the message body.

Support requests will be verified to confirm account ownership and processed promptly (typically within 7 to 30 days depending on identity verification).

3. Data Deleted vs. Retained

Understanding what happens when your account deletion request is completed. Below, "account ID" means the random code that identified your account in our systems; it is not your email address or phone number.

Permanently Erased Data

  • Your sign-in account: email address or phone number, name and sign-in details
  • Your account profile, credit balance and plan status on our servers
  • Videos, audio and images uploaded for AI editing, and the files the AI made from them
  • Our records of each AI edit you ran, and the debug copies of what was sent to the AI and what it returned (except the AI edit plans listed under Data Retained)
  • Our record of the product emails sent to you, including your email address
  • Each AI edit you started, with its request and its result, and the notification tokens of your devices
  • Your email address or phone number in our daily usage records, and your row in the internal reports we rebuild every day (removed at their next daily update)

Data Retained

  • Credit and purchase records: the log of credits granted, spent, refunded and expired, and the Google Play purchase records for anything you bought. We keep them as financial records and so that a purchase cannot be used twice. They stay linked to your account ID, but contain no email address or phone number.
  • A one-way, salted hash of your phone's device ID and, if your account received the welcome credits on that phone, your account ID, so that welcome credits cannot be claimed again on the same phone
  • AI cost records: for each AI request, the feature, the AI model, how much processing it used and when, linked to your account ID
  • AI edit plans from edits made in the style of a reference video, kept to improve our editing: the plan the AI produced and its analysis of the reference video. They contain no videos, file names, email address or text you typed, but they carry a code derived from your account ID, so they are pseudonymous rather than anonymous.
  • App usage analytics linked to your account ID, such as screens and features used and time spent in the app. Google Analytics deletes them within 14 months of your last use of the app. Our copy of these analytics in Google BigQuery and our daily usage records are not currently deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and session recordings of the app's screens (Microsoft Clarity) linked to your account ID, which expire on Google's and Microsoft's own retention schedules
  • Rows in our internal daily usage spreadsheet that were written before you deleted your account, which show your email address or phone number next to that day's app usage. Rows written afterwards show the account as deleted.
  • Emails between you and us, which stay in our mailbox
  • Server logs, which can include your account ID and sometimes your email address, until they are deleted automatically at the end of Google Cloud's log retention period

4. Important Notes

  • Permanence: Account deletion is permanent. Once completed, your saved projects and account settings cannot be recovered.
  • Unsubscribing: Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete your account; you must initiate deletion in-app or submit an email request.

About Zera Studio

Zera Studio is an AI video editing app for Android by ZERA AI. You record or import your own talking video; Zera adds captions, B-roll, music, transitions and a clean voice, and you export the finished reel to your phone. The app uses Google Sign-In only to create your account (your name and email address), and uses Google Cloud AI only to understand the speech and footage in the videos you upload so it can edit them.

Zera Studio edits videos; it does not generate people. It has no feature for making sexual, nude, "undress", face-swap or deepfake content, and using it for non-consensual intimate imagery of anyone is banned under our Terms of Service. Zera Studio is for adults aged 18 and over. See our AI Use Policy for how the AI is used and our Privacy Policy for how your data is handled.
