CapCut alternatives in India, compared honestly

CapCut is not on Google Play or the App Store in India, and capcut.com is unavailable from India[2]. It was reported among the apps India blocked in 2020[1]. If you used it for Reels or Shorts, here is how the editors you can install today compare on what people ask about most: watermark, ads, captions in Hindi and Hinglish, and export quality.

Last checked 13 October 2026Written by ZERA AI, the makers of Zera Studio. We list Zera's weak spots too.

01The short answer

  • Free editing with no watermark: VN, YouCut, Edits and Zera Studio don't add a watermark on their free versions.
  • Hindi captions: InShot, KineMaster, YouCut, Edits and Zera Studio support Hindi captions.
  • Hinglish captions in Roman letters, or AI B-roll and motion graphics for talking videos: these are what Zera Studio is built for.
  • On iPhone: every app here except Zera Studio, which is Android-only for now.

04Where Zera falls short

Honestly.

  • Android only. There is no iPhone version yet.
  • The AI features use credits. Every new phone gets 25 free credits once; after that, AI B-roll and captions need credits from a plan, starting at ₹349 a month. Editing and export stay free.
  • The free version shows ads. Paid plans have none.
  • No template library. Instead, the AI makes motion graphics for the words in each video you give it. If you enjoy browsing ready-made templates, that is a different experience.
  • A young app. It is smaller than the others here and still growing.

05What to look for

No watermark

Export a test video on the free version; don't rely on the store page alone.

Captions in your language

Hindi in Devanagari and Hinglish in Roman letters read very differently on screen. Try both on your own video.

Export quality

1080p is enough for most Reels; 4K and 60 fps help with sharp, fast-moving footage.

Runs on your phone

Edit a real project on the phone you film with, especially if it's a budget phone.

06FAQ

answered.

01Is CapCut available in India?

No. As of 13 October 2026, CapCut is not on Google Play or the App Store in India, and capcut.com is unavailable from India. It was reported among the apps India blocked in 2020.

02Which video editors have no watermark on the free version?

According to their own listings and pages, VN, YouCut, Edits and Zera Studio export without a watermark on their free versions. InShot and KineMaster add one until you pay.

03Which app writes Hinglish captions in Roman letters?

Zera Studio has a Hinglish option that writes Hindi and Hindi-English speech in Roman letters, the way people type it. We found no mention of Roman Hinglish captions on the official pages of the other apps here; several offer Hindi captions in Devanagari.

04What does Zera Studio's AI B-roll do?

It is made for videos where you talk to the camera. Pick a talking video up to 3 minutes and Zera adds stock B-roll, motion graphics, zooms and word-by-word captions, timed to what you say. The edit opens on a timeline, so you can change any part of it before you export.

05Is Zera Studio free?

Editing and export are free (with ads) and never add a watermark. The AI features use credits: every new phone gets 25 free credits once, and plans start at ₹349 a month.

About Zera Studio

Zera Studio is an AI video editing app for Android by ZERA AI. You record or import your own talking video; Zera adds captions, B-roll, music, transitions and a clean voice, and you export the finished reel to your phone.
