06FAQ

Questions,
answered.

01Which languages does it support?

English, Hindi (written in Devanagari) and Hinglish (Hindi and English written in Roman letters). Choose Auto and Zera detects the language from the speech.

02What do the 28 animations look like?

They range from simple to loud: Pop, Wave, Type, Reveal, Letter Drop, Glow, Slide, Scramble, Bounce, Jitter and more. You can see ten of them running on the home page.

03Can I edit the captions?

Yes. Every word can be corrected, and the style can be changed as often as you like without paying again.

04How much do auto captions cost?

1 credit a minute for Auto, English and Hindi, 3 credits a minute for Hinglish, charged by the second. New phones get 25 free credits once.

05Are the captions burned into the video?

Yes. They are part of the exported video, so they show up on every platform you post to.

06Is there a watermark?

No. Exports have no watermark.

About Zera Studio

Zera Studio is an AI video editing app for Android by ZERA AI. You record or import your own talking video; Zera adds captions, B-roll, music, transitions and a clean voice, and you export the finished reel to your phone.
