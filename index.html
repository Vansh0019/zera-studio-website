06FAQ

Questions,
answered.

01What is Zera Studio?

An AI video editor for Android, made for creators who talk to the camera. Pick a video of you talking and the AI adds B-roll, motion graphics, zooms and word-by-word captions, timed to your words. Every AI edit opens in a full multi-track editor where you can change anything.

02Is it free?

Yes. The editor and export are free forever, with ads. Every new phone gets 25 credits once to try the AI features. For more credits and no ads, there are monthly plans from ₹349.

03How many credits does each AI feature use?

AI B-roll uses 15 credits for a 30-second reel, so the Basic plan's 300 credits make about 20 reels a month. Auto captions use 1 credit per minute (3 for Hinglish). Both are charged by the second. The price is shown before you start, and a run that fails gives its credits back.

04Do Hinglish captions come out in Hindi script?

No. Choose Hinglish and the captions are written in Roman letters, the way people type Hinglish. Choose Hindi for Devanagari.

05How long can my video be?

AI B-roll works on talking videos from 4 seconds up to 3 minutes. For a longer recording, cut the part you want in the editor first, then run AI B-roll on it.

06Which phones are supported?

Android phones, through Google Play. An iPhone version is not available yet.

07How do I get support, or delete my account?

Write to support@zera-ai.com. You can delete your account inside the app (Settings) or by following the account deletion page.

About Zera Studio

Zera Studio is an AI video editing app for Android by ZERA AI. You record or import your own talking video; Zera adds captions, B-roll, music, transitions and a clean voice, and you export the finished reel to your phone. The app uses Google Sign-In only to create your account (your name and email address), and uses Google Cloud AI only to understand the speech and footage in the videos you upload so it can edit them.

Zera Studio edits videos; it does not generate people. It has no feature for making sexual, nude, "undress", face-swap or deepfake content, and using it for non-consensual intimate imagery of anyone is banned under our Terms of Service.
