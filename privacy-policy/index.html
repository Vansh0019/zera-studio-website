Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 • Applies to: Zera Studio for Android

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to Zera Studio ("we," "our," or "us"), including the Zera Studio mobile application for Android and this website.

We respect user privacy and aim to maintain clear, transparent practices regarding what information is collected, how it is processed for AI video editing, and how you can exercise your data rights.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with Zera Studio, we collect data in the following categories:

  • Account Credentials: Email address or phone number, authentication tokens, user ID, and account profile details (such as your name from Google Sign-In) when you sign up or log in to the mobile application.
  • User Content & Media: Video clips, audio tracks, images, and text prompts selected or uploaded by you within the app solely to enable AI video editing and timeline creation. Videos you edit only in the manual editor stay on your phone.
  • Credits & Purchases: Your credit balance and a ledger of credits granted, spent and refunded. If you buy a plan, Google Play handles the payment; we receive the purchase record (product, purchase token and order details) to verify it with Google and add your credits. We do not receive your card or payment details.
  • Device Verification: To give free welcome credits once per phone, the app asks Google Play Integrity to confirm the app and device are genuine. We store a one-way, salted hash of a device identifier, not the identifier itself.
  • Notifications: If you allow notifications, we store a notification token for your device, linked to your account, so we can tell you when an AI edit you started is ready. It is removed when you sign out of the app or delete your account.
  • Device & Operational Metrics: Technical device identifiers (such as device model, OS version, app version, and language), usage events (such as which screens and features are used), and diagnostic logs (such as crash reports and editing performance metrics) to maintain app stability and improve the product.
  • Advertising Data: On plans that show ads, Google AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and approximate location to show and measure ads, subject to the consent choices you make in the app.
  • Feedback & Email Correspondence: If you contact us by email, or use the feedback form on our website, we process the contents of your message together with any name and email address you choose to include, in order to read and respond to it. The website feedback form composes the message in your own email application and sends it to us by email; the website itself does not store submissions.
  • Website Visit Counts: When you open a page on this website, it tells our server which page it is, the website you came from (its domain only), the language your browser prefers, whether you are on a phone or a computer and, if you tap a Google Play button, which button. We add these to anonymous daily totals. The website sets no cookies, stores no identifier for you, and does not save your IP address with these totals; our servers' standard request logs, which can include an IP address, are kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting. The website sends nothing when your browser has Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control turned on.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process collected data exclusively for the following operational purposes:

  • Video Editing & AI Assembly: To analyze uploaded footage, detect scenes/cuts, perform automated editing transitions, and generate final video renders.
  • Account & Service Authentication: To maintain secure user sessions, manage account access, and sync project states to your account.
  • Credits, Plans & Abuse Prevention: To charge credits for AI features, give credits back when a run fails, verify purchases with Google Play, and stop free credits from being claimed repeatedly.
  • Application Maintenance & Improvement: To identify technical bugs, resolve crash reports, understand how features are used, and optimize rendering speed.
  • Improving AI Edit: To check and improve the quality of AI Edit, we may keep a small 480p copy of the talking video you upload for AI Edit and of the edit our AI made from it. These copies are used only by us to review and improve AI Edit, are never sold or shared, and are deleted automatically after 30 days, or earlier when you delete your account.
  • Advertising: To show ads in the free version of the app.
  • User Support: To respond to support requests or inquiries sent to support@zera-ai.com.
  • Product Emails: If you sign in with an email address, we send a short welcome email and a few follow-ups during your first two weeks: tips for getting started, a request for feedback, and a check-in if you stop using the app. Every one of these emails has an unsubscribe link, and you can also reply "unsubscribe". We do not send them to accounts that signed in with a phone number only, and we never share your email address with advertisers.

4. AI Processing & Third-Party Infrastructure

Zera Studio utilizes cloud infrastructure and machine learning processing services to deliver AI video editing capabilities:

  • Data Transmission: All data exchanged between the app, web client, and processing services is encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.
  • Third-Party Processing: Data and media processing tasks are handled using cloud service providers bound by contractual confidentiality and privacy obligations. These include Google Firebase and Google Cloud (sign-in, database, file storage, server functions), Google Cloud Vertex AI (Google's Gemini models, which analyze your media for editing, transcription and captions; see our AI Use Policy), Anthropic (Claude models, which plan AI edits from still frames and your transcript and spell captions, used while Google's AI service is unavailable to us; Anthropic does not train on this data and normally deletes it within 30 days), Amazon Web Services (Amazon Transcribe, which turns the speech in your upload into timed words, in the Mumbai region; the audio copy is deleted as soon as the words come back, and within one day at the latest), Google Play (billing and Play Integrity), Google AdMob (ads and consent), Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (usage and crash reports), and Microsoft Clarity (session recordings of the app's screens, used to find usability problems; recording is paused on the sign-in screen).
  • Stock Footage: For AI B-roll, the app searches the Pexels stock library with short search terms chosen by the AI. Your own media is not sent to Pexels.
  • No Data Monetization: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or private video media to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.

5. Data Retention & Storage

Media you upload for AI editing (a reference video, your clips, a talking video for AI Edit, audio for captions) and the files our AI makes from it are used only to make the edit you asked for. They are deleted from our servers automatically within 14 days of upload, and sooner where the edit no longer needs them: a talking video uploaded for AI Edit is deleted once the edit plan is made. The only exception is the 480p review copy described in section 3, which is deleted automatically after 30 days, or earlier when you delete your account. Your projects are stored on your phone, not on our servers. The request and the result of each AI edit you start are kept so the app can give you the result even if it was closed while the edit was being made; they are deleted with your account. The credit ledger and purchase records are kept as financial records, including after account deletion, and a hashed device record is kept so that free credits cannot be claimed again on the same phone.

Upon account deletion, your account record and associated user data are removed from our active database in accordance with our Account Deletion Policy.

6. Data Security

We employ standard administrative and technical safeguards—including encrypted transport layers (HTTPS/TLS) and restricted administrative access—to protect user information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

7. Your Choices & Account Deletion

You have the right to inspect, update, or request deletion of your account and personal data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can initiate account deletion directly within the mobile application settings.
  • Web Request: You can request manual account deletion via email or by following the instructions on our Account Deletion Page.
  • Email Opt-Out: Use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any product email, or reply "unsubscribe", and we will stop sending them.

8. Age Requirement & Children's Privacy

Zera Studio is only for people aged 18 or older and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we close it and delete its data. If you believe someone under 18 has provided us with personal information, please notify us at support@zera-ai.com.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated Effective Date.

About Zera Studio

Zera Studio is an AI video editing app for Android by ZERA AI. You record or import your own talking video; Zera adds captions, B-roll, music, transitions and a clean voice, and you export the finished reel to your phone. The app uses Google Sign-In only to create your account (your name and email address), and uses AI services (Google Cloud, and Anthropic and Amazon Web Services while Google's AI is unavailable to us) only to understand the speech and footage in the videos you upload so it can edit them.

Zera Studio edits videos; it does not generate people. It has no feature for making sexual, nude, "undress", face-swap or deepfake content, and using it for non-consensual intimate imagery of anyone is banned under our Terms of Service. Zera Studio is for adults aged 18 and over. See our AI Use Policy for how the AI is used and our Privacy Policy for how your data is handled.
