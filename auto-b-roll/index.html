01What is B-roll?

B-roll is the footage that plays over your talking shot to show what you are describing: the city you mention, the gym, the laptop, the money. It keeps viewers watching through the parts where a talking head alone might lose them.

02Where does the B-roll come from?

From Pexels, a library of stock footage that is free to use. Zera searches it for each moment it picks, so the clips follow what you say.

03Can I replace a clip I don't like?

Yes. Every piece of the edit is a separate item on the timeline, so you can swap a B-roll clip for your own footage, trim it, move it, or delete it.

04Does it work for Hindi or Hinglish videos?

Yes. Zera transcribes English, Hindi and Hinglish speech and plans the edit from it. Captions can be written in English, in Hindi (Devanagari) or in Hinglish (Roman letters).

05How long can my video be?

From 4 seconds up to three minutes for AI B-roll. For longer recordings, cut the part you want first in the editor, then run AI B-roll on it.

06Is there a watermark?

No. Exports have no watermark, on the free plan too.

07Is it available on iPhone?

Not yet. Zera Studio is on Android, through Google Play.
